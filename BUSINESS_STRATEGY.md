# Business Strategy

This file separates the strategy for each main venture so agents do not mix audiences, languages, or priorities.

## Shared Operating Rules

- Truth first.
- Quality first.
- Build assets, not just tasks.
- Automate repeatable work.
- Separate audiences and messages.
- Use systems that can survive when I am not manually pushing them.

## 1. Professor Rafael

### Positioning

Professor Rafael is my educational personal brand. It serves Brazilian teachers, especially subject-specialist teachers, with practical help for classroom management and AI in education.

### Promise

Help teachers reduce classroom conflict, save time, and work with more confidence, while keeping their professional life honest and sustainable.

### Core Audience

- Teachers in Brazil
- Subject-specialist teachers
- Educators who want practical tools, not empty inspiration
- People looking for classroom management and AI support

### Offer Ladder

- Daily content
- Ebook in perpetual sales
- Mentoring
- Speaking engagements
- Future products and workshops

### Message Rules

- Be practical.
- Be humane.
- Be evidence-informed.
- Do not sell martyrdom.
- Do not imply teaching should consume a person's whole life.
- Emphasize time savings, classroom calm, and better quality of life.

### Revenue Logic

Content should create trust, trust should create leads, leads should create product sales, mentoring, and talks.

### Current Priority

Revenue matters now. The brand should move from attention to cash flow as fast as possible without lowering quality.

## 2. Silva.ai / Soul Anchored

### Positioning

Silva.ai is the AI-assisted content studio I run with my brother. Soul Anchored is one of the main properties inside that studio.

### Audience

- International, English-speaking viewers
- People searching for spiritual, prayer, devotional, and motivational content
- Viewers who respond to polished, AI-assisted, reference-based content

### Promise

Use AI and strong references to create the best possible spiritual content for a given audience segment.

### Operating Model

- Study proven channels and formats.
- Use reference material and scripts as source material.
- Produce high-quality, English-first output.
- Scale with automation and agents wherever reliable.
- Protect quality and consistency.

### Revenue Logic

The goal is to turn content production into a repeatable asset engine with better leverage than manual work.

## 3. Separation Rules

- Do not mix audiences.
- Do not mix languages.
- Do not copy the voice of one brand into the other.
- Professor Rafael is Portuguese-first.
- Silva.ai / Soul Anchored is English-first.
- Professor Rafael is about education and teaching.
- Silva.ai / Soul Anchored is about spiritual content production.

## 4. Strategic Priority Ladder

When a decision affects revenue, health, or family stability, prefer the option that creates long-term leverage and does not damage quality.

## 5. Agent Instructions

- If you are working on Professor Rafael, optimize for practical value to teachers, clarity, trust, and monetization.
- If you are working on Silva.ai / Soul Anchored, optimize for English-language performance, reference fidelity, and production leverage.
- If the task is ambiguous, ask before guessing.
