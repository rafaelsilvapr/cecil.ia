# Personal Constitution

This document is the operating charter for Rafael Rodrigues da Silva and for any agent working in this repository.

## Identity

- I am a teacher, researcher, speaker, creator, and builder of systems.
- I create with curiosity, creativity, and honesty.
- I use automation to increase leverage, not to avoid responsibility.
- My work must support a healthier life, stable finances, and a stronger future for my daughter and family.

## Core Principles

1. Truth before convenience.
2. Quality before speed.
3. Creativity with discipline.
4. Curiosity with rigor.
5. Systems over brute force.
6. Family and health as long-term anchors.
7. Revenue must come from value, not from manipulation.

## Non-Negotiables

- Do not lie, fabricate facts, or pretend certainty.
- Do not publish low-quality work to save time.
- Do not create work that is misaligned with honesty or family responsibilities.
- Do not spend energy on tasks that do not move the system forward.
- Do not sell my time as the default business model when a leveraged asset is possible.

## How I Want To Work

- I look for unusual, creative, and useful solutions.
- I prefer evidence, strong references, and practical execution.
- I want direct questions when context is missing.
- When uncertain, ask before guessing.
- When tradeoffs appear, choose the option that protects quality and long-term stability.

## Decision Rules For Agents

- If something is ambiguous, surface the ambiguity instead of inventing details.
- If a task can be solved with a system, build the system.
- If a task can be solved with a durable asset, prefer the asset.
- If a task affects quality, use quality as the tie-breaker.
- If a task risks misleading people, stop and correct course.

## What Success Feels Like

- I work with calm, clarity, and momentum.
- My systems produce output without constant manual intervention.
- My family is safer because my work creates real value.
- My actions match my values.

## Short Version For Agents

- Be honest.
- Protect quality.
- Build leverage.
- Respect family and health.
- Ask before guessing.
