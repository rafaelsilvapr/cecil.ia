> - **Fonts:** Bold sans-serif (**Montserrat ExtraBold** / **Montserrat Black**).

## Archetype 1: 🌅 Morning Prayer / Start Your Day

**When to use:** Videos about morning routines, starting the day with God, daily devotionals, encouragement to begin the day.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Palette:** Warm tones — orange, golden brown, white. Medium to bright luminosity.
- **Background:** Sunrise landscapes, soft golden light, nature. NO dark scenes.
- **Face:** Generally NO face. Atmospheric/landscape imagery preferred.
- **Text style:** Imperative/action verbs — "START", "PRAY FIRST", "BEGIN", "BLESS YOUR DAY".
- **Text color:** White primary, yellow highlight for the action word.
- **Text position:** Lower third (face is rarely present, so bottom placement is safe).

**Reference thumbnails:**
- `01_above_inspiration_16.6M_morning_blessings.jpg` — 16.6M views
- `02_dailyeffectiveprayer_11.5M_morning_prayer.webp` — 11.5M views
- `03_grace_for_purpose_8.5M_best_morning_prayers.webp` — 8.5M views

**Key patterns from top performers:**
- Above Inspiration dominates this niche with clean, minimal text over warm backgrounds
- DailyEffectivePrayer uses slightly busier layouts but still warm-toned
- Grace For Purpose adds subtle human silhouettes but keeps faces secondary

## Archetype 2: 🌙 Sleep / Night Prayer

**When to use:** Videos about bedtime prayers, falling asleep with God's word, night protection, relaxation with scripture.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Palette:** Extremely dark — black dominant, accents in deep blue/teal or warm brown. Low luminosity.
- **Background:** Night sky, moonlight, dark rooms, candles. Must convey "rest" and "calm".
- **Face:** Sometimes present, but always in low-key/dark lighting. Never bright.
- **Text style:** Comfort words — "SLEEP", "FALL ASLEEP", "LATE NIGHT", "GOODNIGHT", "REST".
- **Text color:** White or soft blue-white. Yellow sparingly (only for the key word like "SLEEP").
- **Text position:** Center or lower third. Dark gradient is essential for readability.

**Reference thumbnails:**
- `01_grace_for_purpose_5.9M_night_prayer_sleep.jpg` — 5.9M views
- `02_grace_for_purpose_3.1M_play_every_night.webp` — 3.1M views
- `03_grace_for_purpose_3.1M_late_night_prayers.webp` — 3.1M views

**Key patterns from top performers:**
- Grace For Purpose dominates this niche entirely
- Near-black backgrounds are the gold standard
- "SLEEP" or "NIGHT" always appear prominently in the text
- Human presence optional but when used, always silhouette or shadow-style

## Archetype 3: 🔥 Motivational Speech

**When to use:** Videos featuring well-known speakers, motivational sermons, celebrity faith testimonies, inspirational life stories.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Palette:** Cinematic — gray, black, brown. Medium to dark. Dramatic lighting.
- **Background:** The speaker's face IS the background. Close-up or medium shot.
- **Face:** ALWAYS present — and it must be the DOMINANT element. The face sells the click.
- **Text style:** Short, punchy hooks — "PUT GOD FIRST", "LIFE IS SHORT", "TRAGEDY INTO TRIUMPH".
- **Text color:** White primary with yellow/gold highlight. Bold, thick font.
- **Text position:** Varies — often to the side of the face or overlaid on a darker region.

**Reference thumbnails:**
- `01_above_inspiration_39.3M_denzel_washington.webp` — 39.3M views
- `02_above_inspiration_9.9M_billy_graham.webp` — 9.9M views
- `03_above_inspiration_9.5M_inky_johnson.webp` — 9.5M views

**Key patterns from top performers:**
- Above Inspiration owns this niche with 39M+ views on the Denzel video
- Celebrity/recognizable faces always outperform anonymous imagery
- Text is secondary to the face — 2-4 words maximum
- Channel branding is subtle or absent on the thumbnail itself

## Archetype 4: 📖 Biblical Stories / Educational

**When to use:** Videos explaining Bible stories, theological concepts, prophecy, angels, demons, historical biblical figures.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Palette:** Dramatic, almost cinematic — black, dark brown, magenta/pink accents. Theatrical lighting.
- **Background:** Artistic/biblical imagery, dramatic scenes, ruins, heavenly imagery. NOT real photos.
- **Face:** Mixed — sometimes artistic biblical figures, sometimes symbolic imagery.
- **Text style:** Curiosity-driven clickbait — "The Man Who...", "Why No One...", "4 Signs...", numbers + mystery.
- **Platform Layouts:**
  - **YouTube (16:9):** 1280x720. Text usually right or left aligned, opposite the main subject.
  - **TikTok (9:16):** 1080x1920. Text centered in the lower-middle half, avoiding UI elements (safe zones).
- **Text color:** White or gold on dark. High contrast mandatory.
- **Text position:** Centered or overlay. Must read like a movie poster.

**Reference thumbnails:**
- `01_grace_digital_8.0M_second_coming.webp` — 8.0M views
- `02_grace_digital_5.7M_three_heavens.jpg` — 5.7M views
- `03_grace_digital_5.5M_archangel_michael.jpg` — 5.5M views

**Key patterns from top performers:**
- Grace Digital Network dominates this niche
- Numbers in text are POWERFUL ("4 Signs", "3 Men", "40 Facts")
- Dramatic cinematic quality — these look like movie posters
- Color accent on dark backgrounds (pink/magenta/gold) creates visual "edge"

## Archetype 5: 🎹 Worship Music / Piano Instrumental

**When to use:** Videos of worship music, instrumental piano, soaking prayer music, meditation soundscapes.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Palette:** The DARKEST of all — near-black with minimal warm accents (brown, dark pink).
- **Background:** Abstract, minimal — piano keys, candles, soft bokeh lights, dark textures.
- **Face:** RARELY present. The mood is the protagonist, not a person.
- **Text style:** MINIMAL text. Only theme title ("IN YOUR PRESENCE", "BE STILL") + DURATION ("3 HOUR", "1 HOUR").
- **Text color:** White or soft warm tone. Never yellow/bold.
- **Text size:** Smaller than other archetypes. Understated.
- **Text position:** Center of frame. Elegant, not aggressive.

**Reference thumbnails:**
- `01_dappytkeys_50.8M_alone_with_god.webp` — 50.8M views
- `02_dappytkeys_13.5M_holy_spirit.jpg` — 13.5M views
- `03_dappytkeys_11.3M_be_still.webp` — 11.3M views

**Key patterns from top performers:**
- DappyTKeys dominates entirely with 50M+ on the top video
- Duration is a KEY selling point (people want long loops)
- Visual minimalism = premium feel. Less is more.
- Channel branding integrated subtly into thumbnail

## Archetype 6: 🧠 Anxiety / Overthinking / Trust

**When to use:** Videos about anxiety relief, trusting God's timing, letting go of control, finding peace, overcoming worry.

**Visual Rules:**
- **Palette:** Medium/neutral — gray, white, black. Clean and modern. Not overly dark or bright.
- **Background:** Landscapes, cloudy skies, serene nature, abstract light. NOT faces (empathy through atmosphere).
- **Face:** Generally NO face. The visual represents the viewer's emotional state.
- **Text style:** Mirroring the viewer's pain — "STOP OVERTHINKING", "TRUST GOD'S TIMING", "LET GO", "GOD IS IN CONTROL".
- **Text color:** White dominant. Yellow/gold for the KEY emotional word.
- **Text position:** Centered or lower third. Clean and organized.

**Reference thumbnails:**
- `01_above_inspiration_7.1M_let_go_trust.webp` — 7.1M views
- `02_above_inspiration_5.6M_trust_gods_timing.jpg` — 5.6M views
- `03_above_inspiration_5.5M_give_it_to_god.jpg` — 5.5M views

**Key patterns from top performers:**
- Above Inspiration dominates this niche too
- Text mirrors the viewer's internal struggle — creates instant empathy
- Visual calm (never chaotic) — the thumbnail itself IS the answer
- Modern, clean aesthetic — appeals to younger demographics

## How The Agent Should Use This Guide

1. **Classify the video** into one of the 6 archetypes based on its content/mood.
2. **Load the 3 reference thumbnails** from the corresponding folder.
3. **Apply the visual rules** (palette, face presence, text style, position) specific to that archetype.
4. **Generate the background image** using a prompt that matches the archetype's background description.
5. **Render text** using the archetype-specific text rules (amount of words, color, position, size).
