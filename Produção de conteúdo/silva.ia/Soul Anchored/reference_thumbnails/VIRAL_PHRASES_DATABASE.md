### Thumbnail Text Rules
- **YouTube thumbnail:** 2–5 words MAX on the image
- **TikTok thumbnail:** 3–6 words MAX (vertical allows slightly more)
- **Video title:** 8–15 words (complements the thumbnail, never duplicates)

### Title ↔ Thumbnail Relationship
> [!IMPORTANT]
> The thumbnail text is NOT the title. They are a PAIR:
> - **Thumbnail** = The HOOK (short, emotional, visual punch)
> - **Title** = The CONTEXT (full description, keywords, SEO)
> 
> Examples:
> - **Thumb:** `PUT GOD FIRST` → **Title:** "Put God First - Denzel Washington Motivational Speech"
> - **Thumb:** `STOP OVERTHINKING` → **Title:** "God Wants You to Stop Overthinking and Trust in His Plan"
> - **Thumb:** `FALL ASLEEP` → **Title:** "Night Prayer Before Sleep | Be Blessed With Psalm 121"
> - **Thumb:** `ALONE WITH GOD` → **Title:** "Alone With GOD: 3 Hour Piano Worship Music for Prayer"
